How To Protect Yourself During an Earthquake

  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On immediately when shaking starts.
  • Stay away from windows, mirrors, and heavy objects that may fall.
  • Use strong furniture like a table or desk for protection.
  • Prepare an emergency kit with water, food, flashlight, and first aid.
  • Secure shelves, TVs, and heavy furniture to walls before earthquakes happen.
  • Know safe places in your home, school, or workplace.
  • If outdoors, stay away from buildings, trees, and power lines.
  • If in a car, stop safely and remain inside until shaking stops.
  • After the earthquake, check yourself and others for injuries.
  • Be ready for aftershocks, they can also be dangerous.
  • If you are in Turkey, call emergency number 112.
