A fast, multilingual guidance system for foreign visitors during disasters in Turkey.

What Should You Do?

  • Call 112 in emergencies.
  • Move to a safe place.
  • Follow official instructions.
  • Get and share reliable information.
  • Ask for help if needed.

Important Emergency Numbers

112 - Emergency / Ambulance

110 - Fire Department

156 - Gendarmerie
