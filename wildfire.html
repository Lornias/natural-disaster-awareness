A wildfire is an uncontrolled fire that spreads rapidly through forests, grasslands, or other natural areas. Wildfires can be caused by extreme heat, drought, lightning, or human activities. They destroy forests, wildlife, homes, and can threaten human lives.

Major Wildfires in Turkey

2021 Manavgat Wildfire

One of the worst wildfires in Turkey’s history. Thousands of hectares of forest burned. Many homes were destroyed and people were evacuated.

2021 Marmaris Wildfire

Severe fire spread quickly due to strong winds. Large forest areas were destroyed. Firefighters worked for days to control the fire.

2023 Çanakkale Wildfire

Fast-spreading wildfire forced evacuations. Many villages were affected. Emergency teams responded quickly to protect lives.
