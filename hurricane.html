Hurricane-Like Storms in Turkey (Medicanes)

2018 Medicane – Zorbas

Affected the Mediterranean region. Caused heavy rain, flooding and strong winds in southern Turkey.

2020 Medicane – Ianos

Impacted the Aegean Sea. Brought intense rainfall and storm damage to western coastal areas.

2023 Mediterranean Storm

Severe storm system brought extreme rainfall to southern Turkey. Led to flooding and infrastructure damage.
